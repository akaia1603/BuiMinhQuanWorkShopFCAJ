## Step 2 — Upload and enable static hosting

1. Upload `index.html` to the bucket.

![Uploading files to the bucket](/images/5-Workshop/5.4-S3-CloudFront/04-upload-files.png)

*Uploading `index.html` to the bucket.*

2. **Properties → Static website hosting → Enable**, Index document: `index.html`.

![Static website hosting enabled](/images/5-Workshop/5.4-S3-CloudFront/05-static-hosting-enable.png)

*Static website hosting: Enabled.*

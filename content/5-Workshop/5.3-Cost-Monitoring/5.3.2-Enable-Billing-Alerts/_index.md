## Step 2 — Enable billing alerts

1. Switch the Region to **`us-east-1` (N. Virginia)**.
2. Go to **Billing Preferences** and enable **Receive Billing Alerts**.

![Billing Preferences with Receive Billing Alerts enabled](/images/5-Workshop/5.3-Cost-Monitoring/04-billing-preferences.png)

*Billing Preferences: `Receive Billing Alerts` is enabled.*

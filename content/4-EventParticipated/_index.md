During my internship, I participated in the **AWS Vietnam Community Meetup** in Hanoi, organized by the AWS Vietnam community in collaboration with the First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ) network. The event connected classroom learning with real industry perspectives on AI, cloud architecture, and career development.

### [Event 1](4.1-Event1/)  
&emsp;**Event name:** AWS Vietnam Community Meetup — AI Trends & Agentic Architectures on AWS  

&emsp;**Date & time:** Saturday, 25/07/2026 – 08:30 to 12:00  

&emsp;**Location:** AWS Hanoi – 7th Floor, Grand Terra Tower, 36 Cát Linh, Đống Đa, Hanoi  

&emsp;**Role:** Attendee  

&emsp;**Summary:** A half-day community meetup with sessions on open-source AI agents (OpenClaw), turning AI trends into business value, shipping fast with AI, and selecting the right AI agent patterns on AWS — plus networking, a Kahoot quiz, and lucky-draw giveaways.
